Clinical Authority & Lead Trainer

Dr. Jason Glowney

Dr. Glowney is board-certified in Internal Medicine and Sports Medicine, and holds a Certificate of Added Qualification in Sports Medicine (CAQSM). He completed a Sports Medicine fellowship at the University of Colorado and has more than ten years of post-fellowship experience in interventional orthobiologics.

He is the founder of Boulder Biologics and its affiliated Boulder Biologics Research Center, where he serves as principal investigator on cellular-therapy trials. He has personally treated thousands of patients across PRP, prolotherapy, and BMAC — the hands-on case volume that grounds everything we teach.
