This Cookie Policy explains how Orthobio Partners LLC (“Orthobio Partners,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on the website at www.getorthobio.com (the “Site”). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve performance, and to provide information to site operators. Cookies may be “session” cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or “persistent” cookies, which remain until they expire or you delete them.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly, such as remembering your preferences and supporting basic security and navigation. The Site cannot operate as intended without them, so they are not optional.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics sets cookies that collect information such as your IP address (often truncated or anonymized), the pages you view, how long you spend on the Site, and the site or source that referred you. This helps us measure traffic and improve our content. The information generated is processed by Google; for more information, see Google’s privacy and terms pages.

3. How to Control Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Browser settings: most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the Site functions.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: you can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available from Google.
  • Do Not Track: some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no industry-standard response to these signals, and the Site does not currently respond to them.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies described above, such as those used by Google Analytics, are set by third parties. We do not control these cookies, and their use is governed by the third party’s own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use or for legal or operational reasons. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date above and post the updated version on this page.
