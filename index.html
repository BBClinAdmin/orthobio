What you can offer your patients

Four treatments. Your choice of any or all.

Autologous biologics come down to four point-of-care procedures, each using the patient's own biology. A practice can offer any combination — the mix is always yours to decide.

PRP

Platelet-rich plasma

The patient's own platelets, concentrated in a centrifuge and injected to support healing in tendons, ligaments, and joints.

Prolo

Prolotherapy

Targeted injections that prompt the body's own repair response in worn or lax ligaments and tendons — using the patient's own healing.

BMAC

Bone marrow aspirate concentrate

Cells drawn from the patient's own marrow and concentrated, then injected where more regenerative capacity is needed.

mFAT

Microfragmented adipose tissue

The patient's own fat, gently microfragmented and injected as a living scaffold rich in regenerative cells — most often paired with BMAC.

What we can offer you

Your patients want this. Building it right is the hard part.

Autologous biologics are one of the most attractive cash-pay service lines in musculoskeletal care. But most practices that want to add them have no clear, reliable path — the barriers are real, and they're hard to clear piece by piece.

The right equipment

Centrifuges, kits, and lab instrumentation take specialized knowledge to spec and size correctly.

A protocol library

Building clinical SOPs, consent, and safety frameworks from scratch is time-intensive and easy to get wrong.

A compliant space

Designing a sterile procedure room and lab to environmental standards requires real, hands-on experience.

Physician technique

Learning to perform these procedures takes the case volume and mentorship a single practice can't self-generate.

We'll partner with you to walk through it all — start to finish.
